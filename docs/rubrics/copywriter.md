# Copywriter rubric (L3)

- Source of truth: `positioning.json` (offer, price, scope, objections) and `brand/voice.md`. Don't change the offer in copy.
- Prose follows Vercel's writing guidelines (`.claude/skills/writing-guidelines`): sentence-case headings, active voice, no dashes as punctuation, lists introduced with a colon, acronyms spelled out first, no vague quantifiers, "we" only for the business's own actions, a one-paragraph summary at the top of each page.
- Buttons and nav labels use Title Case (`web-design-guidelines`); curly quotes and `…` everywhere.
- Every factual sentence carries a claim marker; front matter needs `title`, `nav`, `description`, `path` and `contentType`.
- Never: testimonials, logos, client counts, "trusted by", certifications, guarantees, "FTC approved", a track record the founder doesn't have, or anything in the brief's `flagged_claims`.
- Outreach: personal, under 150 words, a CAN-SPAM postal address and opt-out line, sent only by the founder.
