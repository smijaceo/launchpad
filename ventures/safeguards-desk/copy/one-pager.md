# Safeguards testing check: one page

Countersign Security runs a fixed-fee $490 check for tax, accounting and mortgage offices with 3 to 20 staff on Microsoft 365, and writes up a dated report you can keep in your Safeguards file. <!--c:price-490-->

## Why offices ask for the check

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Safeguards Rule names tax preparation services, accountants who complete tax returns, and mortgage brokers as financial institutions. <!--c:fsr-covers--> For offices with customer information on 5,000 or more consumers, the rule calls for annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments at least every six months, unless the office has effective continuous monitoring. <!--c:fsr-testing--> <!--c:fsr-5000-->

## What the $490 covers <!--c:price-490-->

The fixed fee covers five parts:

- **Scoping call**: consumer count, systems, who owns what, and which parts of the rule likely apply
- **External vulnerability scan**: internet-facing services your office owns or has written permission to test
- **Microsoft 365 review (read-only)**: multi-factor authentication (MFA), admin roles, mail forwarding and inbox rules, former staff accounts
- **Dated report**: findings ranked by risk, with fix steps for your IT person or managed service provider (MSP)
- **Walkthrough**: a 30-minute call

## What the check doesn't cover

The check isn't a full penetration test, a compliance certificate or legal advice, and it doesn't include fixing findings. Testing starts only after you sign a scope and authorisation, and any third party that owns part of your setup signs too.
