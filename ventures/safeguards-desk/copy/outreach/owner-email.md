# Owner outreach emails

The founder sends these personally, at most 10 a day, each edited for the recipient. Every email carries the postal address and opt-out line that CAN-SPAM requires. Fill `first_name`, `office_name` and `personal_line` by hand; never send an unedited template.

## Email 1: first contact

Subject: Testing for {{office_name}}'s Safeguards file

Hi {{first_name}},

{{personal_line}}

I run Countersign Security, a one-person firm that checks small tax, accounting and mortgage offices against the testing side of the FTC Safeguards Rule. <!--c:founder-solo--> If your office holds customer information on 5,000 or more consumers, the rule calls for annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments every six months, unless you have effective continuous monitoring. <!--c:fsr-testing--> <!--c:fsr-5000-->

I'm offering a fixed-fee $490 check: an external vulnerability scan and a read-only Microsoft 365 review, with a dated report for your file. <!--c:price-490-->

Would a 20-minute call in the next two weeks be useful? Even if you don't need the check, I'd value hearing how you handle this today.

{{founder_name}}
Countersign Security · {{postal_address}}
If you'd rather not hear from me again, reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.

## Email 2: follow-up after 5 business days

Subject: Re: Testing for {{office_name}}'s Safeguards file

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up once. If testing isn't on your list, a one-line reply on what you do today (a written plan only, your IT provider's monitoring, or something else) would help me a lot.

{{founder_name}}
Countersign Security · {{postal_address}}
Reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
