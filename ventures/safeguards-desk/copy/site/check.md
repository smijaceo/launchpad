# What the $490 Safeguards testing check includes <!--c:price-490-->

This page lists everything in the check, what it leaves out, what you need to have in place before it starts, and how your data is handled. The price is a fixed $490 for offices with 3 to 20 staff on Microsoft 365. <!--c:price-490-->

## Offices the check is built for

The check fits office owners who hold customer information and want evidence from outside the office, rather than a self-assessment, that their basic safeguards work. It fits best if you keep records on 5,000 or more consumers, because for those offices the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Safeguards Rule calls for annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments at least every six months, unless the office has effective continuous monitoring. <!--c:fsr-testing--> <!--c:fsr-5000-->

To estimate your consumer count, include past years' clients or borrowers whose information you still keep. The scoping call goes through it with you.

## Everything included in the price

Each part of the check, and what it covers:

| Part | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Scoping | A short call and questionnaire covering consumer count, systems, vendors, who owns what and your insurer. You learn which parts of the rule likely apply; your attorney confirms. |
| External vulnerability scan | A scan of the internet-facing services your office owns, such as the website, remote access and email gateway, for publicly known vulnerabilities and risky exposure. |
| Microsoft 365 review (read-only) | Multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every account, admin roles, external mail forwarding and inbox rules, former staff and inactive accounts, and security defaults or conditional access, as far as your licence allows. The rule requires MFA for anyone accessing your information systems unless your Qualified Individual approves an equivalent control in writing. <!--c:fsr-mfa--> |
| Dated report | Findings ranked by risk, with plain fix steps for your IT person or managed service provider (MSP). You can keep it in your Safeguards records. |
| Walkthrough | A 30-minute call to go through the report. |

## Work the check leaves out

The check doesn't include:

- A full penetration test, or internal network, workstation or phishing testing
- Fixing the findings, which your IT person or MSP does using the report's steps
- Legal advice, or any statement that your office is compliant
- Testing anything whose owner hasn't agreed in writing
- Ongoing monitoring after the report

## What you need before the check starts

Four things need to be in place first:

- **Signed scope and testing authorisation**: no scanning or testing happens without it
- **Third-party consent**: if your tax software runs on a hosted desktop, or an MSP or internet provider owns your firewall, that owner agrees in writing; otherwise the system stays out of scope and the report says so
- **Temporary read-only Microsoft 365 access**: your administrator grants it and it's removed at delivery
- **A date that suits your calendar**: tax offices are scheduled around filing deadlines

## How your data is handled

Countersign Security collects only what the report needs:

- Email content is never read, and client tax returns, Social Security numbers and loan files are never stored
- The report and working notes are kept only as long as your engagement letter states, and deleted when you ask
- Your findings belong to you and are shared with your MSP or anyone else only on your instruction
