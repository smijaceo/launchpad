# Safeguards testing your clients can buy through you

If you're a managed service provider (MSP) for tax, accounting or mortgage offices, you can offer the Safeguards testing check under your own brand. Countersign Security does the testing and writes the report, and you keep the client relationship and the remediation work.

For offices that hold customer information on 5,000 or more consumers, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Safeguards Rule calls for annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments at least every six months, unless the office has effective continuous monitoring or other systems that detect, on an ongoing basis, changes that may create vulnerabilities. <!--c:fsr-testing--> <!--c:fsr-5000--> The pilot check gives your clients a dated external vulnerability scan and Microsoft 365 review; it isn't a penetration test.

## How the pilot works for your MSP

The pilot runs on 5 terms:

- **Your brand on the report**: Countersign Security runs the scan and the Microsoft 365 review and writes the report, and you present it
- **Wholesale pricing**: $343 per check during the pilot, 30% below the $490 list price <!--c:msp-wholesale-->
- **Your stack stays yours**: no endpoint protection, backup or monitoring to compete with yours; the report describes the client's configuration, and its fix steps are work you can bill for
- **Clean authorisation**: each client office signs a testing authorisation, and you sign for any assets you own
- **Who sees what**: the client owns the findings and agrees in writing that you receive them; under the pilot terms you get the draft at the same time as the client, so you can plan the fixes

## What a pilot conversation covers

A first call covers which of your clients might need the check, what your current tools already cover, and whether a letter of intent for around 10 offices makes sense for you.
