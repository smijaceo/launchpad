# Pricing for the Safeguards testing check

The check has one fixed price with no subscription and no automatic renewal. Managed service providers (MSPs) in the pilot pay a wholesale price.

## Safeguards testing check
<!--plan:check-->

$490 <!--c:price-490-->

Paid once, before the testing window. For tax, accounting and mortgage offices with 3 to 20 staff on Microsoft 365.

- Scoping call
- External vulnerability scan
- Read-only Microsoft 365 review
- Dated report ranked by risk
- 30-minute walkthrough

## MSP pilot
<!--plan:msp-->

$343 per check <!--c:msp-wholesale-->

During the pilot, 30% below the $490 list price. For managed service providers (MSPs) that serve these offices. <!--c:msp-wholesale-->

- The same check for each client office
- Your brand on the report
- Fix steps you can bill for
- Client-owned findings, shared with you on the client's written terms

## What the price doesn't include

The fixed price excludes:

- Fixing findings
- Full penetration testing
- Internal or phishing testing
- Legal advice
- Ongoing monitoring

Payment is due before the testing window starts. If you need the check repeated on a schedule, raise it on the scoping call.
