# Pricing for the Safeguards testing check

The check has one fixed price with no subscription and no automatic renewal. Managed service providers (MSPs) in the pilot pay a wholesale price.

| Option | Price | Who it's for | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safeguards testing check | $490, fixed, paid once <!--c:price-490--> | Tax, accounting and mortgage offices with 3 to 20 staff on Microsoft 365 | Scoping call, external vulnerability scan, read-only Microsoft 365 review, dated report and a 30-minute walkthrough ([details](/check/)) |
| MSP pilot | $343 per check during the pilot, 30% below list <!--c:msp-wholesale--> | MSPs that serve these offices | The same check for each client office, under your brand ([how the pilot works](/msp/)) |

## What the price doesn't include

The fixed price excludes:

- Fixing findings
- Full penetration testing
- Internal or phishing testing
- Legal advice
- Ongoing monitoring

Payment is due before the testing window starts. If you need the check repeated on a schedule, raise it on the scoping call.
