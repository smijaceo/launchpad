# Who runs Countersign Security

Countersign Security is a one-person business: the founder does every check personally, under written authorisation and at a fixed price. <!--c:founder-solo-->

## Why the business is called Countersign

To countersign a document is to add a second, independent signature that confirms it. Your written security plan states your safeguards, and the check is that second signature: an outside tester confirms what's in place and records what was found.

## The founder's background

The founder's qualifications and hands-on experience:

- Master's degree in cybersecurity <!--c:founder-creds-->
- CompTIA PenTest+, CySA+, Security+, Network+, A+ and Project+ certifications <!--c:founder-creds-->
- Hands-on work in Windows administration and internals, penetration testing tools and Windows detection engineering <!--c:founder-skills-->

Countersign Security is new and taking its first pilot clients. The site will show client names or quotes only after real clients agree in writing.

## Rules the business follows

Every engagement follows the same four rules:

- A fixed price, stated up front, in plain language
- No testing without written authorisation, including from anyone else who owns part of your setup
- A straight answer when you don't need the check
- No legal advice: the report states what was found, and your attorney interprets the rule for your office
