# Questions about the Safeguards testing check

These answers cover scope, price, who the rule applies to, MSPs, hosted software and your data. For anything not covered here, book a scoping call.

## Is the check a penetration test?
No. The check is an external vulnerability scan plus a read-only review of your Microsoft 365 settings. It's called a testing check so nobody mistakes it for a full penetration test. If you need a penetration test, raise it on the scoping call.

## Does the check make my office compliant with the Safeguards Rule?
No check can promise that. The report is evidence of what was checked, on what date, and what was found. Your attorney decides whether your overall program meets the rule.

## Does the Safeguards Rule apply to my office?
The rule names tax preparation services, accountants who complete tax returns, and mortgage brokers as financial institutions. <!--c:fsr-covers--> Its testing schedule, annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments at least every six months, doesn't apply to offices that hold customer information on fewer than 5,000 consumers. <!--c:fsr-5000--> <!--c:fsr-testing-->

## My office is under 5,000 consumers. Should I buy the check?
Not for compliance reasons. The Internal Revenue Service's (IRS) free Publication 5708 helps you write the plan itself. <!--c:irs-5708--> Some smaller offices still want an outside look at their email setup, which is your choice rather than a requirement.

## My managed service provider (MSP) already monitors my office. Do I still need the check?
Possibly not. The rule accepts effective continuous monitoring as an alternative to the testing schedule. <!--c:fsr-testing--> Ask your MSP what they monitor and whether they can give you a dated record. If they want help with the testing side, point them to the [MSP pilot](/msp/).

## My tax software runs on a hosted desktop. Can it be tested?
Only with the hosting provider's written permission. Without it, the hosted system stays out of scope and the report says so.

## Do you need my passwords?
No. Your Microsoft 365 administrator grants temporary read-only access, which is removed at delivery. Nobody reads your email.

## What does the check cost?
A fixed $490. <!--c:price-490--> If you want the check repeated on a schedule, raise it on the call.

## How long does the check take?
You agree a date on the scoping call. The date depends on when the authorisation is signed and on your filing calendar.

## What happens if the check finds signs of an intrusion?
You're told the same day rather than at report time. Call your cyber insurer's hotline or your attorney first: they decide what has to be reported and to whom. For reference, the rule requires notice to the FTC within 30 days of discovering certain events that involve 500 or more consumers. <!--c:fsr-500-->
